Moto Shorts Cloud

Moto Shorts Cloud is a private creator-operated YouTube publishing workflow for the GUSTAVA channel. It prepares original video assets, enforces technical and editorial review, checks the rights to footage, and uploads approved media directly to YouTube using the official YouTube Data API.

The production target is 15 genuinely distinct Shorts per day when enough quality-checked videos are available. The system does not guarantee uploads, audience growth, monetization, or unlimited YouTube access.

How Google authorization is used

The creator authorizes the connected YouTube channel using OAuth 2.0. The tool requests permission to read the channel identity and upload videos. These permissions are needed to verify the account and avoid posting to the wrong channel.

The account owner controls every permission. The app does not ask for a Google password.

Transparency and controls

Direct uploads are blocked unless the channel, source video, quality checks and rights are verified. Scheduling and published status are confirmed through actual YouTube video identifiers. The creator can revoke Google OAuth access at any time.
